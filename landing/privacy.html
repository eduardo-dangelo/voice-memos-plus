Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 5, 2026

This policy describes how Voice Memos Plus ("the app") handles information when you use the mobile app for iOS and iPadOS. It is meant to be read together with Apple's App Privacy details on the App Store product page, which remain the official summary required for distribution once the app is listed.

Information we do not collect in the app itself

Voice Memos Plus does not require you to create an account. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or postal address for app sign-up. The app does not include first-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs as shipped in our codebase.

Recordings and projects stored on your device

Audio you record, layer metadata, folders, and project files are stored locally on your device. We do not operate a Voice Memos Plus cloud account that uploads your memos for us. If you export, share, or back up files using system share sheets or other apps you choose, those destinations follow their own privacy practices.

Microphone access

The app requests microphone permission so you can record memos and overdubs. Microphone audio is processed on device for recording and playback. We do not stream your microphone input to our servers.

Optional location for memo titles

If you grant location permission, the app may use your approximate location to suggest place-based titles for new recordings (similar to Apple Voice Memos). Location is used for that naming feature on your device. You can deny or revoke location access in iOS Settings; the app continues to work without it.

App Store privacy summary (plain language)

For distribution on the App Store, we disclose data practices in Apple's privacy questionnaire. In summary for the practices described above:

  • Audio recordings: Stored on your device for App Functionality. We do not collect them on our servers.
  • Location (optional): Used for App Functionality (suggested memo titles) when you allow access.
  • We do not use this data for Apple's definition of tracking (for example, we do not use it to link your activity across other companies' apps and websites for targeted advertising or advertising measurement, and we do not share it with data brokers for such purposes).

The labels and detail screens shown on the App Store at the time you install the app may include additional categories or updates; they take precedence if this page has not yet been revised.

Children

Voice Memos Plus is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information in a way that concerns you, please contact us.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy, except where applicable law requires additional steps.
